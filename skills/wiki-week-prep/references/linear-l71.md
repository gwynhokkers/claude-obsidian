## Milestones (S2 2026)

**Phases** (weekly prep/class/submit default here):

- `Phase — S2 2026 Foundation` — due ≤ 30 Aug 2026
- `Phase — S2 2026 Mid-term` — 31 Aug – 27 Sep 2026
- `Phase — S2 2026 Post-break` — 28 Sep – 2 Oct 2026
- `Phase — S2 2026 Close-out` — after 2 Oct 2026

**Artefacts** (`Due —` pins; major graded packs only):

- `Due — CSI6208 A1 Number List`
- `Due — MAN6925 Artefact 1 SMIP`
- `Due — MAN5902 Artefact 1 Canvas`
- `Due — CSI6208 Word Find`

Do **not** use `ARCHIVED — Semester 2, 2026 — Year 1 (do not use)`.

## Assignment rules

1. Submit issue for a **major artefact** → that `Due —` milestone
2. Prep/class **feeding** a major artefact → same `Due —`
3. All other weekly work → **phase** by due date (table above)
4. Unit parents → `Phase — S2 2026 Close-out` for the term
